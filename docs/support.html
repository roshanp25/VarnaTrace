Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a subscription on a new device?
Open the app, tap any locked character, then tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscribe screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple, not in the app. Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, or tap "Manage Subscription" from the app's subscribe screen once you're subscribed.

Is my progress backed up anywhere?
Your tracing progress is stored locally on your device only. It isn't backed up to any account, so uninstalling the app will reset it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an account to use the app?
No — there's no login or account system required.
